LEADERSHIP & VISION

From The Principal's Desk

"Success means having the courage, the determination, and the will to become the person you believe you were meant to be." — George Sheehan

Smt. Chandibhai Himathmal Mansukhani College is a reality envisioned by its founding fathers. In 1965, the College was initially established with an objective of satisfying the educational needs of the migrant Sindhi community, but today it stands tall as an inclusive sanctuary of knowledge catering to students of all communities, religions, and socio-economic backgrounds.

We take immense pride in nurturing more than 10,000 learners under one vibrant roof, guided by a committed faculty team of over 400 dedicated educators. Our curriculum embraces NEP 2020 frameworks, research-driven pedagogy, modern IT laboratories, and robust campus placements.

The HSNC Board Legacy & Management

After being displaced from Sindh during Partition in 1947, Principal K. M. Kundnani and Barrister H.G. Advani arrived in Mumbai with a mission to resurrect higher education. Today, the HSNC Board oversees over 25 prestigious institutions across Mumbai and Thane, with Smt. CHM College being its crown jewel in Ulhasnagar.

Vision & Mission: Nurturing global excellence grounded in Sindhi cultural heritage and universal human values.
