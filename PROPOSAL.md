### 1. Executive Summary

Higher education institutions in Maharashtra face mounting administrative complexity driven by the **National Education Policy (NEP 2020)**, **NAAC Cycle 4 accreditation standards**, and the **Maharashtra Public Universities Act 2016**. 

Currently, Smt. CHM College relies on disjointed legacy systems:
1. University MKCL portal for basic admissions (cumbersome, external dependencies, zero branding).
2. Physical paper attendance registers (vulnerable to proxy attendance and time-consuming manual tallying).
3. Third-party SMS gateways and fragmented fee collection channels.
4. Decentralized faculty record-keeping, leading to severe audit stress during NAAC Peer Team visits.

**The Solution:** This project delivers a unified, proprietary, enterprise-grade digital campus operating system comprising **21 interconnected modules**, client-side multi-agent intelligence (**ChandiBot Voice & Text AI**), progressive web app (**PWA**) offline resilience, and containerized deployment (**Docker NGINX Alpine**).

### 4. Commercial Procurement Options

We offer three flexible procurement and deployment structures tailored to the HSNC Board’s administrative preferences:

#### Option A: Perpetual Institutional Enterprise License (Recommended)
- **Model:** One-time capital acquisition of 100% source code, deployment scripts, and intellectual property.
- **Includes:** Complete repository ownership, on-premises Docker installation on CHM College campus servers, 1 full year of priority maintenance, and faculty onboarding workshops.
- **Hosting:** Campus Data Center / Local Private Server (Zero recurring monthly cloud fees).

#### Option B: Annual Managed Campus Cloud (SaaS)
- **Model:** Fully hosted, managed, and monitored cloud subscription.
- **Includes:** High-availability edge hosting, automated weekly backups, 99.9% uptime SLA, security patch management, and unlimited student/faculty user seats.

#### Option C: HSNC Board Multi-Campus Master License
- **Model:** Board-wide rollout across sister institutions (K.C. College, H.R. College, National College, CHM College, Bombay Teachers' Training College).
- **Includes:** Multi-tenant architecture, centralized HSNC Board management dashboard, and shared alumni/recruiter exchange.
