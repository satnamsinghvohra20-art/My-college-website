4-Year Undergraduate Program (FYUP) Multiple Entry-Exit Architecture

Per Mumbai University NEP 2020 guidelines, students can pause or graduate with recognized credentials at each stage.

YEAR 1 (Sem I & II) 40-44 Credits
Undergraduate Certificate

Foundational Major courses, Basic Science/Commerce skills, 4-credit summer vocational internship.

YEAR 2 (Sem III & IV) 80-88 Credits
Undergraduate Diploma

Core Major specializations, Minor discipline initiation, Skill Enhancement (SEC), and Community Engagement.

YEAR 3 (Sem V & VI) 120-132 Credits
Bachelor's Degree (Standard)

Advanced Major mastery, Minor completion, Capstone industrial project. Eligible for state & national civil services.

YEAR 4 (Sem VII & VIII) 160-176 Credits
Bachelor's Degree (Honors / Research)

Ph.D.-track Dissertation, Research Publications, Advanced Seminars. Direct entry into 1-Year Master's or Ph.D. programs.
