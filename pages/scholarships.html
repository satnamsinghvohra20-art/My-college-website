Prominent Higher Education Schemes

100% Tuition & Exam Fee Waiver

Post-Matric Scholarship for SC / ST Students

Sanctioned by Social Justice and Special Assistance Department, Govt. of Maharashtra. Covers complete academic tuition, lab fees, and monthly maintenance allowance.

Income Ceiling: Up to ₹2.50 Lakhs | Portal: MahaDBT
50% Tuition Fee Concession

Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulkh Yojna (EBC)

For economically weaker general category students enrolled in aided or self-financing professional undergraduate courses.

Income Ceiling: Up to ₹8.00 Lakhs | Portal: Directorate of Higher Education (DHE)
Up to ₹30,000 / Year Hostel Aid

Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh Vasatigruh Nirvah Bhatta Yojna

Provides direct annual hostel and mess maintenance subsistence for children of marginal landholder farmers and registered laborers.

Income Ceiling: Up to ₹8.00 Lakhs | Portal: MahaDBT DHE
Special Trust Endowment

HSNC Board Sindhi Minority Merit-cum-Need Freeship

Exclusive scholarship funded by the Hyderabad (Sind) National Collegiate Board for meritorious and economically disadvantaged Sindhi community learners.

Merit Basis: 70%+ in previous qualifying exam | Desk: CHM Trust Office
