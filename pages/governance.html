Procedure to File RTI:

  1. Draft your application on plain paper specifying information required under the RTI Act, 2005.
  2. Enclose prescribed court fee stamp of ₹10/- or Demand Draft in favor of "The Principal, Smt. CHM College".
  3. Submit at the Administrative Dispatch Counter (Counter No. 3) or dispatch via Registered Post.
