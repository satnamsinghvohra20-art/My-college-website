ARCHITECTS OF HSNC BOARD

The Founding Fathers of Smt. CHM College

🏛️

Late Principal K. M. Kundnani

Co-Founder, HSNC Board & Visionary Educationist

Former Principal of D.G. National College, Hyderabad (Sind). In 1949, amidst the turmoil of Partition, he spearheaded the revival of the HSNC Board in Mumbai to ensure Sindhi youth never lost their educational birthright.

⚖️

Late Barrister Hotchand G. Advani

Co-Founder & President, HSNC Board

Eminent jurist and philanthropist whose legal acumen and philanthropic devotion acquired land in Ulhasnagar (Military Barracks Camp 3) to establish CHM College in 1954, transforming a refugee settlement into a premier seat of learning.

🌸

Smt. Chandibhai H. Mansukhani

Benevolent Matron & Chief Benefactor

Whose family's generous endowment in memory of her philanthropic spirit laid the cornerstone of CHM College, inaugurating a legacy that has now educated over 2,50,000 graduates across Science, Arts, and Commerce.

CLASSICAL SINDHI CANON

Digitized Sindhi Literary & Sufi Manuscripts

CENTRAL ARCHIVE MS #001

Shah Jo Risalo — Sur Kalyan (शाह जो रिसालो)

Composed by Hazrat Shah Abdul Latif Bhittai (1689–1752)
پَہِريں ڪَرَ سَڄَڻَ کي، صَلاحَ سَڀَ ڪَنهِين،
تان ڪِيءَ سَڀَ سَنوارِين، جان نه اِھو آئِين.
पहिरीं कर सजण खे, सलाह सभ कन्हीं,
तां कीं सभ संवारीं, जां न इहो आईं।
English Translation: "First seek counsel from the Divine Beloved in every step; unless you arrive at His sanctuary, how can your soul's restless wanderings find equilibrium?"
VEDANTIC SUFISM MS #002

Sami-a-ja Salok (सामी-अ जा सलोक)

Composed by Bhai Chainrai Bachani 'Sami' (1743–1850), Shikarpur (Sind)
समी साहिबु सोई, जेहिं सभु कंहिं जो भारु लयो,
अंदरि बाहरि एकु सो, दूई दूरि कयो।
English Translation: "Sami says: The Supreme Lord is the one who bears the burden of the universe; seeing the One within and without, all delusion of duality vanishes."
RESILIENCE & REBIRTH (1947–1954)

Partition Oral History Archive: The Ulhasnagar Odyssey

AUGUST 1947 — THE GREAT EXODUS

Departure from Karachi & Hyderabad (Sind)

Thousands of Sindhi families boarded steamships at Keamari Port (Karachi) bound for Bombay, leaving ancestral lands and centuries of enterprise behind, carrying only their heritage and devotion to education.

1948 — TRANSIT BARRACKS CAMP NO. 3

Rehabilitation at the Kalyan Military Camp

The Government allocated former WWII British military barracks in Kalyan Camp to 1,00,000+ refugees. On 8th August 1949, Governor-General C. Rajagopalachari christened the township as Ulhasnagar ("City of Joy").

1954 — CONSECRATION OF CHM COLLEGE

Laying the Foundation Stone Opposite Railway Station

Principal K. M. Kundnani and Barrister H. G. Advani inaugurated Smt. CHM College to provide free and subsidized higher education to refugees, ensuring Ulhasnagar became the educational capital of the Sindhi diaspora.
